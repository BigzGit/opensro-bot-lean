# Buff weapon swapping explains itself
- The Buffs & imbue page now lists every buff assigned to a weapon set and what it is waiting for: how many seconds an active buff has left (the swap only happens when it is about to end), that it is due now, that the skill is not learned, or why it was skipped.
- When a buff is due but the swap cannot start, the page says why instead of staying silent: a saved set whose weapon or shield cannot be found in the bag (or two copies look identical), a missing combat weapon, no free bag slot, no saved set, or no buff assigned to a set.
- A buff whose cast was only unconfirmed is tried again after two minutes. Before, it was skipped until you changed buff settings, so a single missed confirmation could stop swaps for good.
- The buff weapon status is part of the diagnostics export, so a bug report shows what the swap was waiting for.

# Classic window and Max every skill
- **Classic window:** a third layout, opt-in with the Classic button at the top of the panel (screens at least 900 px wide). It is a fixed-size window laid out like SBot: a menu bar, HP, MP and EXP bars with the bot status beside them, three wide buttons (Start / stop, Return & hunt, Stop all), the tabs down the left, and your session numbers, supplies and Start / Stop training always on the right. The tabs are Settings, Skills, Items/Storage, Training place, Party/Autores, Player buffing, Auto alchemy and Extra, then Map, Wardrobe and Setup. They hold the same settings as the compact and wide pages, so a change made in one window shows in the others. Compact brings the small window back.
- **Max every skill:** a new button under Settings → Auto mastery and skills (the Extra tab in the Classic window). It learns and upgrades every skill, level by level, including skill lines you have not started, as fast as the game answers. It raises a mastery only when that mastery is what blocks a skill, ignores the gap and reserve settings, and when nothing more is allowed it stops and says what is left (skill points, strength or intelligence). Stop ends it early.
- The compact window reserves less space for its status lines, so it is no longer taller than before, and the town, walk-back and reconnect messages show in the waiting note only. Fixes the panel running off the bottom of short screens in 1.2.115 and 1.2.116.

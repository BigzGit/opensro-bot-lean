# Alt accounts join the main account's party
- **Join the main account's party:** a new option on the Party page (Party/Autores in the Classic window) for your other accounts. Tick **Join the main account's party** and type the main character's name. While the character is not in a party, the bot accepts a party invitation from the main, or finds the main's listing on the party-matching board and applies to it. It does nothing while the character is in another party, and the status line says what it is doing (looking for the listing, applying, party full, level outside the listing's range).
- On the main account, tick **Auto-recruit** and **Auto-accept party-matching joins** so the listing is there and the applications are answered. Leave the new option off on the main account.
- It also brings an account back into the party after a disconnect: once automatic reconnect has the character back in the game, the party is rejoined the same way.

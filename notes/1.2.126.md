# Sell from the character bag on town trips
- New switch in Scroll town cycle: **Sell items from the character bag too**. When on, the town trip sells unprotected items from your own bag at the repair merchant, after the pet loot, by the same rules as pet loot. Off by default.
- Never sold from the bag: potions, scrolls, supplies, Keep names, buff-set gear, the alchemy weapon, and anything with a Store, Keep or Drop rule. A Sell rule always sells.
- Tested in simulation only; not yet confirmed on the live server.

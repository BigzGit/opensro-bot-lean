# The Alchemy tab fuses Luck, Steady and Immortal stones
- New **Magic stones for the weapon** section in Alchemy: choose how many Luck, Steady and Immortal stones the weapon should have (up to 6 each; 0 leaves one off).
- **Fuse stones now** only fuses them. **Start alchemy** fuses the missing stones first, then enhances with elixirs as before.
- Luck raises the enhancement success chance, Steady stops durability damage and Immortal stops destruction when an enhancement fails. The stones must be of the weapon's degree and in your bag.
> It stops if a stone is missing, the game refuses a fusion, or after 30 fusions. New in this version and checked against a simulated game: if a fusion does not raise the count, tell us on Discord.

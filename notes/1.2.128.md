# Town trips no longer stop on an item the merchant won't buy
- A town trip could stop at the weapon merchant with "Inventory rejected: 2". The bot had tried to sell an item the game does not allow merchants to buy (quest and event items, for example), and one refusal ended the whole trip.
- Items the game marks unsellable are now never offered, even with a Sell rule. They stay in the pet or bag.
- If the merchant still refuses an item, the bot keeps it, logs a warning and sells the rest, then carries on with the trip.
- Written from the code and simulated game state; not yet seen on a live server.

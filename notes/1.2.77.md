# Arrows and bolts are bought on town trips
- New **Arrows and bolts** section in Supplies. Tick Buy arrows and/or Buy bolts and set when to go to town and what to refill to.
- They are bought at the repair merchant, after selling pet loot and before repairing. The count includes the stack in your ammunition slot and spare stacks in your bag, and a trip also starts when it falls to your low-stock number.
- If the merchant does not sell the item, there is no bag space or not enough gold, that item is skipped with a notice and the rest of the trip goes on.
> Equipping the bought ammunition is still up to you. Both options are off by default.

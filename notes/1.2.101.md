# Safer obstacle pursuit and confirmed escape recovery
- Blocked enemies are evaluated before repeated attack approaches. Failed routes retain movement control, stop and release the target, and use bounded retries with short-lived failed-approach memory.
- Detours use cached routes and short collision-checked steps. Party damage and position jitter no longer count as a successful detour, and ranged skills get a bounded trial instead of an assumed walking line-of-sight rule.
- Escape uses validated breadcrumbs or legal nearby steps, retries refused local sends, and requires fresh, stable arrival followed by a second move before resuming. No legal exit means a bounded stop instead of blind movement.
- Settings adds optional recovery recall and local detailed escape diagnostics, both off by default. Recovery recall completes town services and stops in town rather than reversing into the trap.
> Live tree-contact recovery still needs controlled gameplay validation. This release does not change OpenSRO collision rules or provide a server-side teleport.

# The imbue is cast during other skills and no longer wasted
- A skill takes the weapon imbue when its cast ends, and the game accepts an imbue while another skill is being cast. The bot waited for the current skill to finish first, so a skill could land without the imbue while the imbue icon was ready.
- The imbue is now cast as soon as it runs out, also in the middle of another skill's cast.
- The Renew imbue when seconds left setting from 1.2.74 is removed: an imbue's cooldown is as long as the imbue itself, so it could never renew earlier, and it only made coatings recast early and waste MP.
